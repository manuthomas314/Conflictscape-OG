Deciphering Conflicts maps documented community opposition to mining across India — Adivasi and farmer resistance, Gram Sabha rejections, public-hearing boycotts, road and rail blockades, dharnas, hunger strikes, displacement and land-acquisition disputes — as live pins on an interactive map of India's coal, bauxite, iron ore, sandstone, diamond, chromite, uranium and sand mining belts. Aggregated from NDTV, India.com, BBC News, Times of India, India Today, Republic, Hindustan Times, Mongabay India, The Hindu and Down To Earth, from 2018 to the present, and updated every six hours.

About Deciphering Conflicts

Deciphering Conflicts is an automated system that reads ten Indian and international news outlets — NDTV, India.com, BBC News, Times of India, India Today, Republic, Hindustan Times, Mongabay India, The Hindu and Down To Earth — every 6 hours and maps reports of community protest against mining. No automated pipeline is perfect — articles may occasionally be missed or unrelated ones may slip through.
