Conflictscape

Environmental conflicts in India, read from the press

A conflict is not a sector. It is a practice coming apart — and it comes apart in one of six ways.

Conflictscape reads ten national news outlets for reported environmental conflicts in India, and files each one by the element of social practice it disrupts: the materials a community lives from, the competences that let it defend them, or the meanings that say what a place is for. Every record links back to the article it came from. Browse by type, by state, and by outlet below.

The inclusion rule

What counts as a conflict here

Two things must be present before an article becomes a record: something environmental at stake, and somebody contesting it. A clearance granted, a pollution reading published, a policy announced — none of these is a conflict until people object. The pipeline looks for both, in the headline and the feed summary, and drops everything else.

Kept

  • Place-based environmental conflicts — a named site, a named community
  • Contestation by the affected: protest, dharna, gherao, gram sabha refusal, a public hearing disrupted
  • Contestation through institutions: a petition, an NGT plea, a stay sought
  • National environmental policy protests — the EIA notification, the forest amendment — flagged separately so site-level cases can be read alone

Not kept

  • Labour and privatisation disputes at the same sites — wages, retrenchment, disinvestment
  • Agricultural market policy, including the 2021 farm laws movement: a dispute over mandis and support prices, not over an environment
  • Wildlife-crime enforcement — a seizure, an arrest — which is policing, not community conflict
  • Disaster and accident reporting where nobody is contesting anything

The typology · click a type to filter the whole page

Six types, one practice disruption each

The type is decided by what the conflict does to practice, not by the sector of the actor involved. That is why a plantation company and a state land bank land in the same type: enclosing farmland disrupts the same configuration of materials and meanings whoever does the enclosing. The three squares beside each type read materials · competences · meanings, filled for the elements that rupture.

The seventh type that isn't

Water is a tag, not a type

Water conflicts in India are numerous and distinct enough to defend as a seventh type — river diversion, irrigation rights, dam displacement, groundwater drawdown. The reason they are not one here is that they do not share a practice disruption. A bottling plant drawing down an aquifer removes a resource. A canal reallocating irrigation water breaks an agrarian practice. A reservoir submerging villages is built infrastructure. Putting all three in one bucket would file them by substance rather than by what they do — the one thing this typology refuses to do everywhere else.

So water runs as a cross-cutting tag. Every water conflict keeps its practice type and is countable as water, and the spread across types is itself a finding rather than something the categories hide.

Where the conflicts are reported

Read this as reported conflict, not as conflict: a state with an active regional press and a national bureau will be over-represented against one without.

Method

How a headline becomes a record

The pipeline

  1. Poll the ten outlets' own feeds for the last few days; search Google News with a site: filter per outlet to reach back to 2018.
  2. Apply the conflict gate — an environmental object plus contestation, minus the excluded genres.
  3. Score the text against six keyword lexicons; the highest-scoring type wins, and the gap to the runner-up is stored as the record's margin.
  4. Resolve the place against a GeoNames-derived gazetteer, longest name match first, disambiguated by the state the article names.
  5. Verify every stored link and drop the ones that 404 or bounce to a homepage.

What the numbers are not

  • Not a census of conflict. They are a census of reporting on conflict, by ten English-language outlets.
  • Not one row per conflict. A long-running case generates many articles; each is a record.
  • Not adjudicated. The classifier reads words, not merits. Records assigned on a margin of 1 or less are marked thin margin and are the ones to read by hand.
  • Not exhaustive on place. Articles naming no resolvable place are dropped rather than guessed at.
